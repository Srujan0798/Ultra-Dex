## Sales Process

### Lead Qualification (BANT)
- **Budget**: Does the prospect have allocated budget?
- **Authority**: Is this person a decision maker?
- **Need**: Do they have a clear need for our solution?
- **Timeline**: What is their timeline for implementation?

### Demo Structure (30 minutes)
1. **Introduction** (5 min)
   - Brief company intro
   - Understand prospect's needs
   
2. **Product Demo** (15 min)
   - Dashboard overview
   - Multi-agent coordination
   - Visual debugging
   - Security features
   
3. **ROI Discussion** (5 min)
   - Cost savings examples
   - Productivity gains
   - Competitive advantages
   
4. **Next Steps** (5 min)
   - Trial offer
   - Proposal discussion
   - Timeline alignment

### Objection Handling

#### "Too Expensive"
- Emphasize ROI and cost savings
- Compare to building internally
- Offer annual discount
- Highlight enterprise features

#### "Need to Build Internally"
- Show time-to-value advantage
- Highlight maintenance burden
- Demonstrate advanced features
- Offer custom development

#### "Security Concerns"
- Highlight SOC 2 compliance
- Discuss on-premise options
- Share security documentation
- Offer security review
