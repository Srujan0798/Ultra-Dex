## Risk Management

### Critical Path Risks

1. **Technical Complexity**: Agent orchestration and AI integration challenges
   - **Mitigation**: Experienced team, phased development approach
   - **Owner**: CTO

2. **Talent Acquisition**: Difficulty hiring qualified engineers
   - **Mitigation**: Competitive compensation, strong employer brand
   - **Owner**: CEO

3. **Market Competition**: Rapid response from established competitors
   - **Mitigation**: Clear differentiation, fast execution
   - **Owner**: Head of Product

4. **Customer Adoption**: Slower than expected customer acquisition
   - **Mitigation**: Strong value proposition, effective marketing
   - **Owner**: Head of Marketing

### Contingency Plans

- **Technical Delays**: Additional engineering resources, scope adjustment
- **Market Response**: Pivot strategy, feature adjustment
- **Funding Issues**: Cost reduction, extended runway
- **Competitive Pressure**: Accelerated development, pricing adjustment
