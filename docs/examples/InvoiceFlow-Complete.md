# SECTION 1: HIGH-LEVEL SUMMARY

## 1.1 Product Vision (One-liner)

Automate your invoicing lifecycle from creation to payment collection.

## 1.2 Problem Statement

Freelancers waste hours every month manually creating invoices, chasing late payments, and reconciling bank statements with spreadsheets.

## 1.3 Solution Overview

InvoiceFlow provides a React-based invoice builder, automated PDF generation, recurring billing, and Stripe-integrated payment tracking with automated email reminders.

## 1.4 Target Market

- Independent freelancers (designers, developers, writers)
- Small consulting agencies (2-10 people)
- Service-based small businesses

## 1.5 Unique Value Proposition

- "Zero-Click" recurring invoices
- Automatic late fee calculation and enforcement
- Unified dashboard for tax-ready financial reporting

## 1.6 Success Metrics (Key)

# SECTION 2: CORE FEATURES

## 2.1 Core Production Features (Critical - P0)

**Feature 1: Interactive Invoice Builder**

- Simple Description: Create professional invoices with a live preview.
- Industry Standard: Drag-and-drop line items with dynamic tax calculation.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  □ Support for multiple currencies
  □ Real-time total calculation (Subtotal, Tax, Discount, Total)
  □ Custom branding (Logo and brand colors)

**Feature 2: Automated PDF Generation**

- Simple Description: Generate and store professional PDF invoices.
- Industry Standard: High-quality, downloadable PDF documents.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  □ PDFs generated in < 2 seconds
  □ Secure storage in AWS S3 or Supabase Storage
  □ Mobile-friendly download links

**Feature 3: Stripe Payment Integration**

- Simple Description: Accept credit card and ACH payments directly on invoices.
- Industry Standard: Secure checkout with instant status updates.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  □ Integrated Stripe Checkout session
  □ Automatic invoice status update to "Paid" on webhook trigger
  □ Support for partial payments

**Feature 4: Automated Reminders**

- Simple Description: Schedule email reminders for overdue invoices.
- Industry Standard: Drip sequence for payment follow-ups.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  □ Customizable reminder schedule (3, 7, 14 days overdue)
  □ Email tracking (Open/Viewed status)
