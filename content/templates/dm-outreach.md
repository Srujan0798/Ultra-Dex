# DM Outreach Template

## Template

Hey {{username}},

Saw your comment: "{{comment}}"

Re: {{signal}} - I'm building Ultra-Dex to solve exactly this. We're in early beta and I'd love 10 minutes of your time to understand your use case better.

Worth a quick chat?

Best,
Solo dev building Ultra-Dex

## Variables

- `username` - Reddit username
- `comment` - Their original comment (truncated if long)
- `signal` - Type of signal detected (buying_signal, interest_signal, etc.)
