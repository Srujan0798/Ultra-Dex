# User Interview Script

## Goal

Understand if users actually need AI routing/orchestration.

## Who to Interview

- AI developers using multiple providers
- CTOs/engineers managing AI costs
- Teams switching between OpenAI/Anthropic/Gemini

## Questions to Ask

### Opening

"I'm building a tool that routes between AI providers automatically. Can you tell me about your current setup with AI providers?"

### Problem Discovery

1. "How many AI providers do you currently use?"
2. "How do you decide which provider/model to use for a task?"
3. "What's your biggest pain point with managing multiple providers?"
4. "How do you currently handle provider outages or rate limits?"
5. "How much time do you spend managing AI provider configurations?"

### Current Solutions

6. "Are you using any tools to manage multiple providers? (LiteLLM, OpenRouter, etc.)"
7. "What do you like/dislike about your current approach?"
8. "How do you track costs across providers?"

### Willingness to Pay

9. "If a tool could automatically route to the cheapest adequate model, would you use it?"
10. "Would you pay $50/month for that? What about $100/month?"
11. "Who would make the buying decision for this in your company?"

### Feature Priority

12. "What's the ONE feature that would make you try this immediately?"
13. "What features could we skip?"

### Closing

14. "Can I follow up with you when we have a beta ready?"
15. "Do you know 2-3 other people I should talk to?"

## What to Listen For

**Good signs:**

- "I waste hours switching providers"
- "Cost management is a nightmare"
- "We hit rate limits constantly"
- "I hate managing multiple API keys"

**Bad signs:**

- "I only use OpenAI"
- "My current solution works fine"
- "This seems complicated"
- "Why not just use LiteLLM?"
