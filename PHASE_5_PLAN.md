# Phase 5: Scale & Growth Plan

## Executive Summary

Phase 5 focuses on scaling Ultra-Dex to 5,000+ customers, expanding to international markets, adding advanced AI features, and preparing for Series B funding. This phase transitions Ultra-Dex from a successful launch to a market leader with sustainable growth and advanced capabilities.

## Phase 5 Objectives

### Primary Goals
1. **Customer Scale**: Scale to 5,000+ customers across multiple market segments
2. **International Expansion**: Expand to European and Asian markets
3. **Advanced AI Features**: Implement multimodal AI capabilities and advanced reasoning
4. **Series B Preparation**: Prepare for Series B funding round
5. **Market Leadership**: Establish Ultra-Dex as the market leader in AI-assisted development

### Success Criteria
- 5,000+ active customers by end of Phase 5
- $5M+ ARR with 200%+ year-over-year growth
- International presence with 30%+ of revenue from outside US
- Advanced AI features implemented and adopted
- Series B funding secured for next phase
