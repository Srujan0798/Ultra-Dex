# 📞 PART 4: DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT

## 30-Minute Discovery Call Structure

### **Opening (2 minutes)**

```
"Thanks for taking the time today, [Name].

Before we dive in, I want to make sure I understand your situation.

Can you tell me a bit about your role and what you're responsible for when it comes to AI initiatives?"

[Listen actively, take notes]
```

### **Problem Discovery (8 minutes)**

```
"Got it. So a few questions to understand your current setup:

1. What AI models are you currently using or evaluating?
   [Listen for: GPT-4, Claude, etc.]

2. How are you handling context when switching between them?
   [Listen for: custom code, manual copy/paste, or nothing]

3. What happens when an AI agent fails in production?
   [Listen for: silent failures, no monitoring, manual fixes]

4. How do you handle compliance requirements?
   [Listen for: SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA, audit trails]

5. What's blocking you from getting more AI pilots to production?
   [Listen for: security, reliability, cost, team bandwidth]"
```

### **Current State (5 minutes)**

```
"Thanks for sharing that. Let me summarize what I'm hearing:

• You're using [models] but struggling with [specific problem]
• You've built [current solution] but it's [limitation]
• Your main blocker to production is [compliance/security/reliability]
• If you could solve this, it would mean [business impact]

Is that accurate? What did I miss?"
```

### **Value Proposition (5 minutes)**

```
"Based on what you've shared, here's how Ultra-Dex would help:

[Match their specific problems to your solutions]

For example:
• Cross-model memory → solves your context loss problem
• Agent autopsy → prevents silent failures you mentioned
• SOC 2 compliance → passes your security review
• 99.9% SLA → meets your reliability requirements

Does that align with what you're looking for?"
```

### **Demo (5 minutes)**

```
"Let me show you exactly how this works for your use case.

[Share screen, show relevant demo]

Key points to hit:
1. Their specific use case
2. How it solves their #1 problem
3. Security/compliance features
4. ROI calculation

Pause for questions throughout."
```

### **Next Steps (5 minutes)**

```
"So where do you see this fitting into your roadmap?

Options:
1. Start 30-day pilot (up to 10 users, full features)
2. Technical deep dive with your team
3. Custom proposal based on your requirements
4. Reference calls with similar companies

What makes the most sense for you?"

[Close with specific next steps and calendar booking]
```
