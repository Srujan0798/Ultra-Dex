**Delete immediately:**

- ❌ All 12 apps except one minimal dashboard
- ❌ Agent swarms — no evidence anyone wants this
- ❌ CLI as primary product — developers want SDKs
- ❌ Governance layer — enterprise feature for companies with no enterprises
- ❌ Desktop app — dead code
- ❌ Mobile app — vaporware
- ❌ White-label — premature abstraction
- ❌ 28 CI workflows → keep 3
- ❌ 100+ docs → keep 5
- ❌ Archive folder — delete entirely
- ❌ "Meta-layer" positioning — means nothing

**What could actually work:**

The governance + audit trail infrastructure is the ONLY defensible piece. Companies deploying AI face real compliance requirements. If you pivot to **"AI Compliance as a Service"** — logging every AI interaction, enforcing policies, generating audit reports for SOC2/HIPAA — you solve a real enterprise problem.

### Category Pivot: AI Compliance Infrastructure

**Real problem:** Companies using AI have ZERO audit trails. When regulators ask "what did your AI system decide?", companies have no answer. This is a compliance nightmare waiting to happen.

**New positioning:** "AI Audit & Compliance Layer" — the audit trail infrastructure that makes AI deployments audit-ready.

**Why this could work:**

1. Enterprise buyers understand compliance
2. Regulatory pressure is increasing, not decreasing
3. No dominant player in AI compliance
4. Governance code already exists — repurpose it
5. Defensible because it requires trust + certifications
