## Communication Protocols

### Daily Standup (15 minutes)
- What did you complete yesterday?
- What are you working on today?
- Any blockers or impediments?

### Weekly Team Sync (1 hour)
- Demo completed work
- Review metrics and progress
- Adjust priorities and plans
- Celebrate wins and learnings

### Monthly All-Hands (2 hours)
- Company updates and announcements
- Product demos and showcases
- Customer success stories
- Q&A with leadership

### Quarterly Planning (2 days)
- Review past quarter performance
- Set objectives for next quarter
- Align on company strategy
- Team building and vision exercises

## Onboarding Timeline (First 90 Days)

### Days 1-7: Setup & Orientation
- Complete infrastructure setup
- Attend company orientation
- Meet with key stakeholders
- Review product and documentation
- Set up development environment

### Days 8-30: Deep Dive
- Complete technical training
- Work on starter projects
- Attend team meetings and standups
- Begin contributing to codebase
- Pair with senior team members

### Days 31-60: Contribution
- Take ownership of features/modules
- Participate in design reviews
- Contribute to architecture decisions
- Begin mentoring newer hires
- Set Q2 OKRs with manager

### Days 61-90: Mastery
- Lead feature development
- Contribute to strategic decisions
- Mentor junior team members
- Plan for next role level
- Prepare for performance review
