## Interview Process

### Standard Process (All Roles)

1. **Screening Call** (30 min) - HR/Founder
2. **Technical Screening** (60 min) - Relevant team member
3. **Culture Fit Interview** (45 min) - CEO/CTO
4. **Technical Deep Dive** (90 min) - Relevant team members
5. **Final Interview** (60 min) - CEO/CTO

### Role-Specific Additions

- **Engineering**: Take-home coding challenge (4-8 hours)
- **Sales**: Sales presentation and role-play scenario
- **Design**: Portfolio review and design challenge
- **Marketing**: Content creation sample and strategy presentation

### Evaluation Criteria

- **Technical Skills** (40%): Relevant experience and expertise
- **Cultural Fit** (25%): Alignment with company values
- **Problem-Solving** (20%): Ability to think critically
- **Communication** (15%): Ability to collaborate effectively
