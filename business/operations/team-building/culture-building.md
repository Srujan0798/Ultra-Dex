## Team Integration Process

### Phase 1: Individual Integration (Weeks 1-4)

#### Week 1: Personal Foundation

- **Day 1**: Welcome ceremony and team introductions
- **Day 2**: One-on-ones with direct team members
- **Day 3**: Cross-functional team introductions
- **Day 4**: Mentor assignment and first mentor session
- **Day 5**: Week 1 feedback and goal setting

#### Week 2: Project Integration

- **Day 6-7**: Project assignment and context setting
- **Day 8-9**: First project contributions and feedback
- **Day 10**: Cross-team collaboration initiation

#### Week 3: Process Integration

- **Day 11-12**: Process and procedure deep dive
- **Day 13-14**: Quality standards and best practices

#### Week 4: Independence Building

- **Day 15-16**: Independent project work
- **Day 17-18**: Leadership and initiative demonstration
- **Day 19-20**: Month 1 review and feedback

### Phase 2: Team Integration (Weeks 5-8)

#### Week 5: Team Dynamics

- **Team Building Activity**: Collaborative project kick-off
- **Cross-Team Collaboration**: Inter-team project assignment
- **Knowledge Sharing**: First team presentation

#### Week 6: Leadership Development

- **Mentorship**: Begin mentoring junior team members
- **Initiative**: Lead a small team initiative
- **Feedback**: Provide peer feedback and receive feedback

#### Week 7: Innovation

- **Idea Generation**: Contribute to innovation sessions
- **Process Improvement**: Identify and implement improvements
- **Experimentation**: Lead a small experiment

#### Week 8: Integration Review

- **360-Degree Feedback**: Comprehensive feedback session
- **Goal Setting**: Next phase goal setting
- **Celebration**: Month 2 milestone celebration

### Phase 3: Company Integration (Weeks 9-12)

#### Week 9: Company Culture

- **Values Workshop**: Deep dive into company values
- **Leadership Interaction**: Sessions with leadership team
- **Strategy Alignment**: Understanding company strategy

#### Week 10: Cross-Department Collaboration

- **Interdepartmental Projects**: Work with other departments
- **Process Understanding**: Understand company-wide processes
- **Relationship Building**: Build relationships across departments

#### Week 11: Thought Leadership

- **Internal Presentations**: Present to company-wide audience
- **External Representation**: Represent company externally
- **Knowledge Contribution**: Contribute to company knowledge base

#### Week 12: Full Integration

- **Comprehensive Review**: Full integration assessment
- **Future Planning**: Career and growth planning
- **Milestone Celebration**: 90-day integration milestone
