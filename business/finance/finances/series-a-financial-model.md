## Unit Economics

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
```
CAC Calculation:
• Marketing Spend: $120,000/year
• Sales Team Cost: $200,000/year  
• Total CAC Investment: $320,000/year
• New Customers Acquired: 408/year
• CAC = $320,000 ÷ 408 = $784

CAC Payback Period:
• Average Annual Contract Value: $2,450
• Gross Margin: 85%
• Annual Gross Profit per Customer: $2,082
• CAC Payback Time: $784 ÷ ($2,082 ÷ 12) = 4.5 months
```

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
```
LTV Calculation:
• Average Customer Lifespan: 4.2 years
• Average Annual Revenue per Customer: $2,450
• LTV = $2,450 × 4.2 = $10,290

LTV/CAC Ratio:
• LTV ÷ CAC = $10,290 ÷ $784 = 13.1x
• Industry Benchmark: 3x minimum
• Ultra-Dex Performance: 4.4x above benchmark
```

## Risk Analysis

### Financial Risks
```
High Risk Scenarios:
• Slower customer acquisition (50% slower): Revenue reduced by 40%
• Higher churn (double current rate): Revenue reduced by 25%
• Pricing pressure (20% price cut): Revenue reduced by 20%
• Economic downturn: Revenue reduced by 30%

Mitigation Strategies:
• Diversified customer base to reduce concentration risk
• Strong unit economics allowing for temporary pricing adjustments
• Focus on customer success to minimize churn
• Conservative hiring and expense management
```
