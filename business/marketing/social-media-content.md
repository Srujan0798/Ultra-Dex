**Ultra-Dex Launches Enterprise AI Orchestration Platform with Visual Debugging and Enterprise Security**

_Platform combines multi-agent coordination, visual debugging, and enterprise-grade security to make AI development delightful for Fortune 500 companies_

SAN FRANCISCO, CA – February 14, 2026 – Ultra-Dex, the AI orchestration platform that makes enterprise AI development delightful, today announced its official launch. The platform solves the multi-agent coordination problem with visual debugging, enterprise-grade security, and production-ready infrastructure, making it the only platform that combines enterprise security with delightful developer experience.

Unlike other AI tools that focus on single-agent workflows, Ultra-Dex coordinates multiple specialized AI agents to accomplish complex tasks. The platform provides visual debugging for AI agent execution flows, enterprise-grade security with SSO and audit logging, and production-ready multi-agent coordination.

"We founded Ultra-Dex to solve the multi-agent coordination problem that's holding back enterprise AI adoption," said Srujan Sai Karna, CEO and co-founder of Ultra-Dex. "While others focus on single-agent tools, we're building the infrastructure that enables specialized AI agents to work together seamlessly with visual debugging and enterprise controls."

Ultra-Dex has seen rapid adoption among enterprise customers, with over 500 active users and $5,000+ in monthly recurring revenue growing at 15% month-over-month. The platform is SOC 2 and GDPR compliant with enterprise-grade security features including SSO, RBAC, audit logging, and encryption.

Ultra-Dex's platform features 16 specialized agents that coordinate to accomplish complex tasks, visual debugging with real-time execution flow visualization, and a tiered memory system with hot/warm/cold storage. The platform offers 2-minute setup with guided initialization and provides enterprise-grade security with SSO, RBAC, and audit logging.

The company plans to continue expanding its engineering team and enhancing security and compliance features. Ultra-Dex aims to reach $50,000 in monthly recurring revenue by the end of the quarter.
