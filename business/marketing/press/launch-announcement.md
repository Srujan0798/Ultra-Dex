# Ultra-Dex Launch Announcement

## FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

### Ultra-Dex Raises $15M Series B to Lead the AI Orchestration Revolution

**Enterprise-grade platform coordinates specialized AI agents with visual debugging and production-ready infrastructure, serving Fortune 500 companies worldwide**

**SAN FRANCISCO, CA – February 15, 2026** – Ultra-Dex, the AI orchestration platform that makes enterprise AI development delightful, today announced it has raised $15 million in Series B funding led by a16z AI with participation from existing investors. The funding will be used to accelerate global expansion, enhance the platform's predictive orchestration capabilities, and scale the team to meet growing demand from enterprise customers.

Ultra-Dex has emerged as the leader in the AI orchestration space, helping enterprise teams coordinate specialized AI agents with visual debugging, enterprise security, and production-ready infrastructure. Unlike other AI tools that focus on single-agent workflows, Ultra-Dex solves the real challenge: coordinating multiple specialized agents to accomplish complex tasks.

"We founded Ultra-Dex to solve the multi-agent coordination problem that's holding back enterprise AI adoption," said Srujan Sai Karna, CEO and co-founder of Ultra-Dex. "While others focus on single-agent tools, we're building the infrastructure that enables specialized AI agents to work together seamlessly with visual debugging and enterprise controls."
